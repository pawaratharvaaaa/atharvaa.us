1. OBJECTIVE & ARCHITECTURE
Redesign and transform the personal portfolio of Atharva Pawar (pawaratharvaaaa, thevibecoderguy) into an exact, pixel-perfect match of the publication-grade editorial design and tactile interactivity found on ziad.us.

3. PAGES & ROUTES
- Home: Masthead rule-row, editorial nameplate with animated SVG signature mark, role, bio, pull quote, shortcut chips, In This Issue table of contents (01 Work, 02 Now, 03 Lab, 04 Contact), SEO About aside.
- Work: Seven projects list with numbers, metadata, editorial titles, shipped/ongoing badges (with pulsing live pips), tech stack tags, cursive hand notes.
- Work Detail Pages: Individual deep-dive pages for all 7 projects (musclempire, music-model, Lumen, wedoit, PRANKER, custome_todo, portfolio) featuring stats dl, live/repo links, rotated taped preview cards, margin notes, and multi-section narrative prose (The Problem, The Approach, The Stack, Reflection).
- Now: Dual-pane Tmux terminal frame with ~/now.md (building, on my mind) and ~/ps (live process monitor with PIDs 0101, 0202, 0303, 0404).
- Lab: Terminal experiments view for ~/lab/palette [2026] and interactive playground triggers.
- Contact: Editorial numbered contact channels (GitHub, Email, X/Twitter, Pinterest, Phone) with hover transitions and cursive closing statement.
- Global: Sticky navigation header, live telemetry status bar (LAT, LON, BOM time, scroll px), command palette (` or Cmd+K), theme switcher, CRT scanline mode, Konami code snake easter egg.
